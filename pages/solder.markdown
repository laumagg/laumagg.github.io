# SolderSense

## 2nd Place Award

### 25-29.06.2025 

Mixed reality soldering training created at <a href ="https://www.xrcc.events/" target="_blank">XRCC</a>\| Meta Quest 3, Logitech MX Ink Stylus

**Technologies:** Unity, C#, XR Interaction Toolkit  

**Team:** Markéta Kučerová, Ray Ng Jun Hui, Lorenzo Carly & Artem Kolomatskyi

SolderSense is a mixed reality learning experience designed to teach the fundamentals of soldering through a guided, tutorial-based workflow. Users can practice essential techniques step by step in an immersive environment that blends real-world interaction with digital instruction. 

The main technical highlight implementing the soldering iron as a virtual overlay mapped onto the Logitech MX Ink stylus for Meta Quest 3, resulting in precise, natural interaction. The experience is guided by a voiceover recorded by Anthony Vitillo.

The project was developed in under 70 hours as part of the Education Track by Meta at the XRCC hackathon, where it was awarded 2nd place!
